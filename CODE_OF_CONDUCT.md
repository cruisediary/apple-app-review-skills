# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Acceptable Behaviors

- Using welcoming and respectful language
- Providing constructive, actionable feedback
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them get started
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

## Unacceptable Behaviors

- Harassment or intimidation in any form
- Discriminatory language or jokes related to race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, disability, or other personal characteristics
- Personal attacks or insults directed at other contributors
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or contributions

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may be reported to **cruzdiary@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Depending on severity, violations may result in a temporary ban or permanent removal from the project community.
