# App Store Review Guidelines — Section 3: Business

Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#business

## 3.1.1 In-App Purchase

Apps that offer digital goods or features must use Apple's In-App Purchase (IAP) system. This is non-negotiable for consumables, non-consumables, subscriptions, and access to digital content.

**External payment links are not allowed** — apps may not direct users to external websites or payment systems to complete purchases of digital content.

**Loot boxes and randomized items** — Apps using "loot boxes" or other mechanisms that offer randomized virtual items for purchase must:

> "...disclose the odds of receiving each type of item to customers prior to purchase."

The odds disclosure must appear before the user commits to the purchase, not after. Failure to disclose odds before the transaction is grounds for rejection.

## 3.1.2 Subscriptions

Auto-renewable subscriptions are available to all app categories. Requirements:

- Subscriptions must provide ongoing value to justify recurring charges
- Minimum subscription period is **7 days**
- Free trials are permitted but must be clearly disclosed
- Subscriptions may not lock users out of previously purchased content

### 3.1.2(c) Subscription Disclosure Requirements

Before a user subscribes, the app must clearly describe what the user will receive. Required disclosures that must appear **before** the subscription confirmation:

1. **Billing amount** — displayed prominently (not buried in fine print)
2. **Renewal terms** — how often the subscription renews and at what price
3. **Trial end date** — if a free trial is offered, when it ends and what charges begin
4. **Cancellation method** — how to cancel the subscription (e.g., via Settings > Subscriptions)

> "Discount percentages must not overshadow the actual price the user will be charged."

Displaying a large "50% OFF" while showing the discounted price in small text is a violation. The actual charge amount must be at least as prominent as any promotional framing.
